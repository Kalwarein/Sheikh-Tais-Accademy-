Examination Guidelines

Important rules and guidelines that all students must follow during examinations.

Before the Exam

  • Report to the exam hall 15 minutes before start time
  • Bring valid student ID card
  • Carry required stationery (pens, pencils, ruler, calculator if permitted)
  • Leave bags and electronic devices outside the hall

During the Exam

  • Follow all instructions given by supervisors
  • Do not communicate with other students
  • Raise your hand if you need assistance
  • Remain seated until instructed to leave

Prohibited Items

  • Mobile phones and smart devices
  • Smartwatches and electronic devices
  • Notes, textbooks, or reference materials (unless open-book exam)
  • Calculators (unless specifically permitted for the subject)

Results Declaration

Information about how and when examination results are released to students and parents.

  • Results will be available within 2 weeks of examination completion
  • Check results through the mobile app or school portal
  • Printed report cards available from the school office
  • Parents will receive notification when results are published
  • Grade review requests must be submitted within 5 days of results

Results on Mobile App

Download the Henmat International School mobile app to receive instant notifications when your results are available. View detailed grade breakdowns and track your academic progress over time.

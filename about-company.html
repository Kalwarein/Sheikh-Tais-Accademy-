About OrangeTel

Connecting communities with reliable telecommunications since 2010

Our Story

OrangeTel was founded in 2010 with a simple mission: to make high-quality telecommunications accessible to everyone. Starting with just 3 cell towers and a team of 20 dedicated employees, we have grown to become one of the nation's leading mobile network providers.

Today, OrangeTel serves over 5 million customers across 500+ cities with 5,000+ cell towers providing 98% population coverage. Our commitment to innovation, reliability, and customer satisfaction has made us the preferred choice for individuals and businesses alike.

Our Mission

To connect every person and business with fast, reliable, and affordable telecommunications services, empowering communities to thrive in the digital age.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted telecommunications provider, known for exceptional network quality, innovative services, and outstanding customer care.

Our Values

Reliability: We ensure our network is available 24/7, 365 days a year.
Innovation: We continuously invest in the latest technologies.
Customer Focus: Every decision we make puts customers first.
Integrity: We operate with transparency and honesty.

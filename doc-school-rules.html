School Rules & Regulations

Official policies and guidelines that govern our school community. All students, parents, and staff are required to read and understand these regulations.

Effective: January 2025 20 min read Version 2.0

Table of Contents

Code of Conduct Attendance Policy Academic Rules Disciplinary Actions Dress Code Technology Policy

Important Notice

All students and parents are required to read and understand these rules and regulations. Compliance is mandatory for all members of the Henmat International School community. By enrolling at our school, students and parents agree to abide by these policies.

Code of Conduct

Henmat International School is committed to maintaining a safe, respectful, and conducive learning environment for all students, staff, and visitors. Our code of conduct is built upon core values that define our community.

Core Values

Respect

Treat all members of the school community with dignity and respect, regardless of their background, beliefs, or abilities.

Integrity

Act honestly and ethically in all academic and personal interactions within the school environment.

Responsibility

Take ownership of your actions and their consequences, contributing positively to the school community.

Excellence

Strive for the highest standards in academic achievement and personal development.

Expected Behaviour

  • Show respect to teachers, staff, and fellow students at all times
  • Use appropriate language and maintain professional conduct
  • Resolve conflicts peacefully and seek help from staff when needed
  • Take care of school property and facilities
  • Follow all safety protocols and emergency procedures
  • Participate actively and positively in school activities
  • Report any concerns or incidents to appropriate school personnel

Attendance Policy

Regular attendance is essential for academic success and is required by law. Students are expected to attend all scheduled classes and school activities. Consistent attendance promotes learning continuity and academic achievement.

Attendance Requirements

Attendance Rate Status Action Required
95% and above Excellent Continue maintaining excellent attendance
90% - 94% Good Monitor and maintain current level
85% - 89% Concern Parent meeting required
Below 85% Critical Intervention plan and counselling required

Absence Procedures

  • Parents must notify the school before 9:00 AM on the day of absence
  • Written excuse required within 3 days of return
  • Medical certificates required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days
  • Pre-planned absences must be approved in advance

Tardiness Policy

  • Students arriving after 8:00 AM are considered tardy
  • Three tardies equal one absence in records
  • Excessive tardiness may result in disciplinary action
  • Late students must report to the office before going to class

Academic Rules

Academic Integrity Statement

Henmat International School maintains the highest standards of academic integrity. All students are expected to complete their own work honestly and to properly acknowledge the work of others.

Academic Integrity

Academic integrity is fundamental to learning and personal development. Students must demonstrate honesty in all academic work and assessments.

  • Complete all assignments independently unless group work is specified
  • Properly cite all sources used in research and assignments
  • Avoid plagiarism, cheating, and unauthorised collaboration
  • Report any instances of academic dishonesty witnessed
  • Accept responsibility for understanding assignment requirements

Assignment and Homework Policy

  • All assignments must be submitted on the specified deadline
  • Late submissions may result in grade penalties (10% per day)
  • Extensions may be granted for valid reasons with prior approval
  • Make-up work is available for excused absences only

Examination Rules

Examination Guidelines

Students must arrive on time for all examinations with required materials. Electronic devices are not allowed in examination halls unless specifically permitted. Any form of cheating will result in immediate disqualification and disciplinary action.

  • Report to the examination hall 15 minutes before start time
  • Bring valid student ID and required stationery
  • Electronic devices are not allowed unless specified
  • Follow all instructions given by supervisors
  • Remain seated until instructed to leave

Disciplinary Actions

The school employs a progressive discipline system designed to help students learn from their mistakes and make better choices. Our goal is always to guide students towards positive behaviour.

Minor Infractions

Examples: Tardiness, dress code violations, minor disruptions in class

  • Verbal warning
  • Written warning
  • Parent notification
  • Detention

Major Infractions

Examples: Cheating, disrespect to staff, fighting, vandalism

  • Office referral
  • Parent conference
  • In-school suspension
  • Out-of-school suspension

Severe Infractions

Examples: Violence, drug/alcohol possession, weapons, harassment

  • Immediate suspension
  • Police involvement if necessary
  • Expulsion consideration
  • Legal consequences

Dress Code

Students are expected to dress appropriately for a learning environment. The dress code promotes safety, reduces distractions, and maintains a professional atmosphere conducive to learning.

General Guidelines

  • Clothing should be clean, neat, and in good repair
  • Appropriate fit - not too tight or too loose
  • No offensive language, images, or symbols
  • Shoes must be worn at all times

Uniform Requirements

Shirts/Tops

  • School polo shirts (navy blue or white)
  • School logo must be visible
  • Collared shirts are acceptable

Bottoms

  • Khaki or navy blue pants/skirts
  • Appropriate length (knee-length minimum)
  • No rips, holes, or distressing

Footwear

  • Closed-toe shoes required
  • Athletic shoes for PE classes
  • No flip-flops or sandals

Technology Policy

Technology is an integral part of modern education. Students are expected to use technology responsibly and appropriately, recognising it as a tool for learning and development.

Acceptable Use

  • Educational purposes only during class time
  • Respect intellectual property and copyright laws
  • Maintain appropriate online behaviour
  • Report any technical issues to staff

Mobile Device Policy

  • Phones must be silenced during class
  • Use only when permitted by teacher
  • No recording without permission
  • Violations may result in device confiscation

Internet and Email Guidelines

  • Use school accounts for educational purposes
  • No accessing inappropriate content
  • Respect privacy of others
  • Report cyberbullying or harassment immediately

Privacy and Data Protection

The school is committed to protecting student data and privacy in accordance with applicable data protection laws. Student information is used only for educational purposes and is not shared with third parties without consent.

Questions About Our Policies?

Contact our administration office for clarification on any school rules or regulations. We are here to help.

About the App

The Henmat International School mobile app is designed to keep students and parents connected with the school. Access grades, timetables, announcements, and more from your Android device.

Getting Started

Follow these simple steps to get started with the Henmat International School mobile application. The app is available for Android devices and can be downloaded from our official website.

1

Download the App

Download the Henmat International School app from our official downloads page.

Only download from official sources

2

Install & Launch

Install the APK file on your Android device and launch the application.

Enable "Unknown Sources" if prompted

3

Create Account

Sign up using your email, password, and name. Select your account type (Student, Staff, or Parent).

Fill all required fields

4

Verify & Sign In

Click the verification link sent to your email, then return to the app and sign in.

Check spam folder if needed

System Requirements

The app requires Android 6.0 (Marshmallow) or higher, 50MB of free storage space, and an active internet connection for full functionality.

Installation Guide

Detailed instructions for downloading and installing the Henmat International School mobile application on your Android device.

Downloading the App

  1. Visit the official Henmat International School website
  2. Navigate to the Downloads page
  3. Click the "Download APK" button
  4. Wait for the download to complete

Enabling Installation from Unknown Sources

Important Security Note

Before installing, you may need to enable installation from unknown sources. This is safe when downloading from our official website only.

  1. Go to Settings on your Android device
  2. Tap on Security or Privacy settings
  3. Find "Install from Unknown Sources" or "Install Unknown Apps"
  4. Enable permission for your browser or file manager
  5. Locate the downloaded APK file and tap to install
  6. Follow the on-screen installation prompts

Account Setup

Learn how to create and verify your account to access all features of the mobile application.

Creating Your Account

  • Open the app and tap "Create Account" or "Sign Up"
  • Enter your full name as registered with the school
  • Provide a valid email address (preferably the one registered with school)
  • Create a strong password (minimum 8 characters)
  • Select your account type: Student, Parent, or Staff
  • Tap "Create Account" to submit your registration

Email Verification

Verification Steps

  • Check your email inbox for the verification message
  • Click the verification link in the email
  • Return to the app and sign in with your credentials
  • You're now ready to use all app features

Troubleshooting

Solutions to common issues you may encounter while using the mobile application.

App will not load or crashes frequently

Try clearing the app cache in your device settings, restart your device, or reinstall the app. Ensure you have sufficient storage space (at least 100MB free) and a stable internet connection. If the problem persists, contact technical support.

Cannot log in with my credentials

Double-check your email address and password for typos. Ensure Caps Lock is not enabled. If you have forgotten your password, use the "Forgot Password" option to reset it. If issues continue, contact the school office for credential verification.

Notifications are not working

Check your device's notification settings and ensure the app has permission to send notifications. Also verify your notification preferences within the app settings. Make sure battery optimisation is not blocking app notifications.

App installation fails

Ensure you have enabled "Install from Unknown Sources" for your browser or file manager. Check that you have sufficient storage space. Delete the APK file and re-download it from our official website. Restart your device and try again.

Data not syncing or showing old information

Pull down on the screen to manually refresh data. Check your internet connection is stable. Log out and log back in to force a full data sync. If the issue persists, clear the app cache and restart.

Still Need Help?

If you cannot resolve your issue using the troubleshooting guide above, please contact the school office or send an email to support@henmatschool.edu for technical assistance.

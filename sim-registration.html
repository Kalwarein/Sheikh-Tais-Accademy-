SIM Registration

Register your SIM card to enjoy full network services

Why Register?

SIM registration is a mandatory requirement to ensure network security and enable full access to all OrangeTel services. Unregistered SIM cards may have limited functionality.

Requirements

Valid National ID, Passport, or Driver's License
Passport-size photo (or we can take one at the store)
Your OrangeTel SIM card
Proof of address (utility bill or bank statement)

Registration Process

Simple steps to register your SIM

1

Visit a Store

Find your nearest OrangeTel store or authorized dealer location.

2

Provide Documents

Present your valid ID, proof of address, and passport photo.

3

Biometric Capture

Your fingerprints will be captured for verification purposes.

4

Activation

Your SIM will be activated within 24 hours of registration.

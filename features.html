Our Academic Levels

Comprehensive education from early years to secondary school

Early Years (Ages 3-6)

Foundation learning through play-based education, focusing on early literacy, numeracy, and social skills development.

Key Focus Areas:

  • Language development
  • Basic numeracy
  • Social interaction
  • Creative play

Primary School (Ages 6-12)

Building strong academic foundations with emphasis on literacy, numeracy, sciences, and critical thinking skills.

Key Subjects:

  • English & Mathematics
  • General Science
  • Social Studies
  • ICT & Creative Arts

Junior Secondary (JSS 1-3)

Intermediate education preparing students for senior secondary with specialized subject introduction.

Key Features:

  • Core subject mastery
  • Practical skills
  • Exam preparation
  • Career guidance

Senior Secondary (SSS 1-3)

Advanced academic programs preparing students for WASSCE and university entrance examinations.

Streams Available:

  • Science stream
  • Arts stream
  • Commercial stream
  • Technical studies

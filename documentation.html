Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to commonly asked questions about our school, services, and policies.

How do I get login credentials for the mobile app?

Login credentials are provided by the school administration during the admission process. If you have not received your credentials or need them reset, please contact the school office during working hours or email us at info@henmatschool.edu.

What should I do if I forget my password?

Use the "Forgot Password" option in the mobile app to reset your password. You will receive a password reset link via email. If you continue to have issues, please contact the school office for manual password reset assistance.

How can parents access their child's information?

Parents receive separate login credentials during the admission process. Using these credentials, parents can access their child's academic information, attendance records, grades, and school communications through the mobile app or parent portal.

Is the mobile app available for iOS devices?

Currently, the app is available only for Android devices. An iOS version is in development and will be released in the near future. We will announce the iOS release through our official communication channels.

What are the school timings?

Regular school hours are from 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Students should arrive by 7:45 AM for morning assembly. After-school activities and clubs operate from 3:15 PM to 5:00 PM.

How do I report my child's absence?

Parents must notify the school before 9:00 AM on the day of absence by calling the school office or using the mobile app notification feature. A written excuse is required within 3 days of the student's return. Medical certificates are required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.

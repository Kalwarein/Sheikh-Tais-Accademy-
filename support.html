Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

How do I check my data balance?

You can check your data balance by dialing *100# or through the OrangeTel app. The app shows detailed usage breakdowns including remaining data, calls, and SMS.

How do I buy a data bundle?

Dial *123# and follow the menu prompts to select your preferred bundle. You can also buy data through the OrangeTel app, which offers app-exclusive deals and instant activation.

What should I do if I forgot my PUK code?

Visit any OrangeTel store with a valid photo ID that matches the registered SIM owner. Our staff will verify your identity and provide your PUK code. You can also call customer service at *123#.

How do I register my SIM card?

New SIM cards must be registered within 30 days of purchase. Visit our SIM Registration page or visit any OrangeTel store with your national ID and a passport photo.

Can I transfer data to another OrangeTel user?

Yes! You can share data with other OrangeTel users through the app or by dialing *103*NUMBER*MB#. A small transfer fee may apply. Both users must be on OrangeTel network.

How do I activate international roaming?

Dial *126# to access roaming options or enable roaming through the app settings. We recommend purchasing a roaming package before traveling. Standard pay-as-you-go rates apply if no package is activated.

What are the customer service hours?

Our phone support is available 24/7 by dialing *123# or calling our hotline. Store hours vary by location - use our store locator to find hours for your nearest store.

How do I stop unwanted promotional messages?

Dial *130# and select the option to manage your marketing preferences. You can also opt out through the OrangeTel app under Settings > Privacy > Marketing Communications.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

How do I check my data balance?

You can check your data balance by dialing *100# or through the Qcell app. The app shows detailed usage breakdowns including remaining data, calls, and SMS.

How do I buy a data bundle?

Dial *123# and follow the menu prompts to select your preferred bundle. You can also buy data through the Qcell app, which offers app-exclusive deals and instant activation.

Can I transfer data to another Qcell user?

Yes! You can share data with other Qcell users through the app or by dialing *103*NUMBER*MB#. A small transfer fee may apply. Both users must be on Qcell network.

What is the Night Bundle and how does it work?

Night Bundles provide discounted data that can only be used between 12AM and 6AM daily. They're perfect for downloading large files or streaming at night. Purchase by dialing *123*50#.

What should I do if I forgot my PUK code?

Visit any Qcell store with a valid photo ID that matches the registered SIM owner. Our staff will verify your identity and provide your PUK code. You can also call customer service.

How do I register my SIM card?

New SIM cards must be registered within 30 days of purchase. Visit our SIM Registration page or visit any Qcell store with your national ID and a passport photo.

Can I keep my number if I switch to Qcell?

Mobile Number Portability (MNP) is available in Sierra Leone. Visit any Qcell store with your current SIM and ID to initiate the porting process. It typically takes 24-48 hours.

How do I activate international roaming?

Dial *126# to access roaming options or enable roaming through the app settings. We recommend purchasing a roaming package before traveling. Standard pay-as-you-go rates apply if no package is activated.

How do I recharge my account?

You can recharge using scratch cards by dialing *101*PIN#, through the Qcell app, mobile money, or at any authorized dealer. The app also supports bank card payments.

What are the customer service hours?

Our phone support is available 24/7 by dialing 076-000-000 or calling our hotline. Store hours vary by location - use our store locator to find hours for your nearest store.

How do I stop unwanted promotional messages?

Dial *130# and select the option to manage your marketing preferences. You can also opt out through the Qcell app under Settings > Privacy > Marketing Communications.

Why was my data deducted even though I have a bundle?

This can happen if your bundle expired or you exceeded your data allowance. Check your bundle status with *100#. Some apps may also use data in the background. Enable data saver mode in your phone settings.

Common Issues & Solutions

Quick fixes for common problems

No Internet Connection

  1. Check if mobile data is enabled
  2. Verify you have an active data bundle
  3. Restart your device
  4. Check APN settings (APN: internet)

Poor Network Signal

  1. Move to an open area
  2. Toggle airplane mode on/off
  3. Check for network outages
  4. Try manual network selection

Cannot Make Calls

  1. Check airtime balance (*100#)
  2. Ensure SIM is properly inserted
  3. Disable call barring if enabled
  4. Contact support if issue persists

SIM Locked

  1. Enter correct PIN (3 attempts)
  2. Use PUK code to unlock
  3. Visit Qcell store with ID
  4. Get PUK from customer service

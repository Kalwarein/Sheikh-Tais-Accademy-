About Elevation Lodge

Discover our story, mission, and commitment to exceptional mountain hospitality

Our Story

Elevation Lodge was founded in 2014 with a singular vision: to create a sanctuary where guests could experience the magnificence of nature without sacrificing comfort and luxury. Nestled in the heart of the Alpine Valley, our lodge was built on land that has been treasured for generations.

What began as a family dream has blossomed into one of the region's most beloved mountain retreats. Our founders, avid nature enthusiasts and hospitality veterans, combined their passions to create a space that honors the natural beauty of the mountains while providing world-class accommodations and service.

Today, Elevation Lodge stands as a testament to thoughtful design, sustainable practices, and genuine hospitality. Every detail, from our locally-sourced cuisine to our handcrafted furnishings, reflects our commitment to authenticity and excellence.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide guests with transformative experiences that reconnect them with nature, rejuvenate their spirits, and create lasting memories. We believe that exceptional hospitality goes beyond comfortable rooms and fine dining—it's about creating moments of genuine connection and wonder.

Our Vision

We envision Elevation Lodge as more than a hotel—it's a destination where adventure meets tranquility, where luxury embraces sustainability, and where every guest leaves feeling renewed. Our goal is to set the standard for mountain hospitality worldwide.

Sustainable Practices

Environmental stewardship is at the heart of everything we do. Our lodge operates on 100% renewable energy, sources 80% of our restaurant ingredients locally, and maintains strict conservation practices. We've earned Green Certification and continue to innovate in sustainable hospitality.

Our Accommodations

Each of our 50+ rooms and suites has been thoughtfully designed to maximize comfort and showcase the stunning natural surroundings. From cozy standard rooms perfect for solo travelers to expansive suites ideal for families, every space features premium amenities, handcrafted furniture, and panoramic views.

Culinary Excellence

Our award-winning restaurant showcases the best of mountain cuisine. Executive Chef Maria Santos leads a team dedicated to crafting dishes that celebrate local ingredients and international techniques. From hearty breakfasts to elegant dinners, every meal is an experience to remember.

Adventure & Wellness

Whether you seek adventure or relaxation, Elevation Lodge offers it all. Explore our extensive hiking trails, enjoy world-class skiing in winter, or unwind in our luxury spa. Our concierge team can arrange everything from guided nature walks to private yoga sessions overlooking the mountains.

Awards & Recognition

We're honored to have received numerous accolades, including TripAdvisor's Certificate of Excellence, AAA Four Diamond status, and recognition as one of the Top 10 Mountain Retreats by Travel + Leisure magazine. These awards reflect our team's dedication to exceptional service.

Our Team

Behind every exceptional stay is our dedicated team of hospitality professionals. From our front desk staff to our housekeeping team, our chefs to our adventure guides, every member of the Elevation Lodge family is committed to making your visit extraordinary.

Why Choose Us

Choosing Elevation Lodge means choosing an experience, not just a stay. We offer the perfect balance of adventure and comfort, luxury and authenticity, personal attention and privacy. Here, you'll find a place where the mountains feel like home and every moment matters.
